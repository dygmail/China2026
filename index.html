01 ⛰Our best overall fit
CHANGCHUN → HARBIN

The indoor mountain & city escape

A snack-filled mountain inside a mall, then games, old-town eats and a proper spa day. The best balance of novelty and easy travel.

7–8 Dec Changchun · 9–12 Dec Harbin

For the teens · Novel food stops + a dedicated Harbin arcade afternoon.

For the adults · A relaxed food crawl, Modern brewpub and one bathhouse day.

Explore places & the 6-night plan

这有山 · This Hill, Changchun

An indoor hillside of winding steps, little shops and places to eat. Give everyone a snack budget and explore for 2–3 hours; it is a playful food-and-shopping experience, with plenty of stairs.

桂林路 / 桂林胡同 · Guilin Road

A lively snack district for grilled squid, crispy pork, drinks and dessert. Pick a few small portions to share; in December, use indoor restaurants between short street-food stops.

Harbin play + soak

Use the Haxi arcade and Galaxy bathhouse picks below on separate days, with an early Modern brewpub dinner in between.

A suggested flow · December 2026

  1. 7 · Transfer from Club Med to Changchun; hotel, then This Hill.
  2. 8 · Lazy morning; Guilin Road lunch and Hongqi Street browsing.
  3. 9 · Train to Harbin; check in, Central Street and brewpub dinner.
  4. 10 · Haxi Wanda arcade afternoon and hot-pot dinner.
  5. 11 · Galaxy bathhouse / indoor water-park day.
  6. 12 · Chinese Baroque pastries, favourite-food encore and packing.
  7. 13 · Flight day. Breakfast, hotel checkout and prebooked transfer to HRB. See the shared departure plan below.

Getting around

Arrange one family-sized transfer from BDH to Changchun. Continue by train to Harbin on 9 Dec; allow half a day door to door and choose the actual stations before booking.

Crowds & timing

Popular, not a hidden gem. Visit This Hill before the evening peak if arrival allows, and Guilin Road before the dinner rush.

The trade-off

One hotel change; excellent variety without a big geographical detour.

02 🍦Tried & tested
HARBIN · ONE BASE

Harbin, with the good bits turned up

The postcard city, done around good meals and indoor breaks rather than a checklist of winter attractions.

7–12 Dec Harbin · 6 nights

For the teens · Ice cream, an arcade session and pastry souvenirs.

For the adults · Historic streets, a proper beer-and-food stop and hot springs.

Explore places & the 6-night plan

中央大街 + 圣索菲亚教堂 · Central Street & Saint Sophia

Short walks between European-style façades, the cathedral exterior and warm café stops. Aim for a 60–90-minute wander rather than hours outdoors; this is the busiest, most familiar Harbin choice.

马迭尔精酿啤酒屋 · Modern brewpub

A brewery-style restaurant in the Modern Hotel on Central Street, with German-style dishes and house beer. Adults can taste beer while everyone shares dinner; confirm that the early dining session admits teens.

老鼎丰 · Lao Ding Feng, Jingyu Street

A historic pastry shop with a small pastry museum. Buy a tasting selection to share and compare favourites; a fun, warm stop that makes the old-town walk more than a photo exercise.

A suggested flow · December 2026

  1. 7 · BDH → Harbin; settle in and eat close to the hotel.
  2. 8 · Saint Sophia exterior, Central Street and early brewpub dinner.
  3. 9 · Haxi Wanda games and a shared hot-pot dinner.
  4. 10 · Chinese Baroque neighbourhood and Lao Ding Feng.
  5. 11 · Maple Leaf hot springs; relaxed dinner after returning.
  6. 12 · Free choice: repeat the best food or games, then pack.
  7. 13 · Flight day. Breakfast, hotel checkout and prebooked transfer to HRB. See the shared departure plan below.

Getting around

Transfer via Jilin or Changchun station, then train to Harbin; compare a private through-car quote if you prefer no station changes. Stay central for the full six nights.

Crowds & timing

Central Street gets busy. Visit before lunch; avoid scheduling every dinner on the pedestrian street.

The trade-off

Easiest first visit, but less adventurous than the regional detours.

HARBIN · HAXI + CITY

Claw machines, snacks & rematches

A deliberately unhurried city week with two game sessions, plenty of browsing and room to return to the food everyone loved.

7–12 Dec Harbin · 6 nights

For the teens · Two arcade visits, flexible shopping and their own snack picks.

For the adults · Shared restaurant dinners, one beer evening and a recovery day.

Explore places & the 6-night plan

大玩家（哈西万达店）· Dà Wán Jiā arcade

A listed arcade on level 3 of Haxi Wanda, 168 Zhongxing Avenue. Plan 90–120 minutes per visit. Ask which claw, racing and rhythm machines are currently operating before loading credit; no specific machine or unlimited pass is assumed.

哈西万达广场 · Haxi Wanda

Keep lunch, bubble tea and shopping in the same mall as the games. Let each teen choose one food stop; use a family token budget so the claw-machine hunt stays fun.

A grown-up finish

Add the Modern brewpub for dinner one night and Galaxy for a separate daytime soak. This balances the games without sending the kids off alone while the adults drink.

A suggested flow · December 2026

  1. 7 · Reach Harbin and settle into one hotel.
  2. 8 · Haxi arcade: first round, mall lunch and shopping.
  3. 9 · Central Street dessert crawl; early brewpub dinner.
  4. 10 · Galaxy bathhouse and a quiet evening.
  5. 11 · Arcade rematch; teens choose dinner.
  6. 12 · Lao Ding Feng gifts or the favourite food stop; pack.
  7. 13 · Flight day. Breakfast, hotel checkout and prebooked transfer to HRB. See the shared departure plan below.

Getting around

Haxi is convenient for the games; central Harbin is better for evening walks. Pick one hotel base and group outings by neighbourhood rather than moving hotels.

Crowds & timing

Try the arcade shortly after opening on a weekday. Quieter timing is a planning suggestion, not a crowd guarantee.

The trade-off

Best if the kids genuinely want downtime; fewer landmark visits. Arcade inventory needs a fresh check.

04 ♨Best recovery week
HARBIN · SONGBEI + CITY

Bathrobes, buffets & hot pools

Make the Northeast bathhouse experience the highlight: warm pools, lounging and a meal break, with one arcade day for balance.

7–10 Dec central Harbin · 11–12 Dec Songbei

For the teens · Indoor water play, generous downtime and a city arcade visit.

For the adults · Two different kinds of spa day, with no rushed evening transfers.

Explore places & the 6-night plan

银河欢乐世界 · Galaxy Happy World

An enormous leisure complex at 83 Shimao Avenue combining a water park, bathhouse, dining and rooms. Reserve half a day for indoor facilities. Water-park access, bathhouse access and meals may be sold separately; do not assume outdoor slides run in December.

枫叶小镇温泉 · Maple Leaf hot springs

A hot-spring resort beside the Maple Leaf outlets. Expect pools and slow lounging rather than a spa treatment automatically included in admission. Use indoor pools as the dependable plan and confirm which outdoor pools are operating.

Central-city interlude

Break up the soaking with Haxi games, a pastry stop and a food-led evening. Keep alcohol for after the spa, at dinner.

A suggested flow · December 2026

  1. 7 · Arrive and stay in central Harbin.
  2. 8 · Central Street food walk and Modern dinner.
  3. 9 · Haxi arcade and mall lunch.
  4. 10 · Chinese Baroque and an easy evening.
  5. 11 · Move to Songbei; Galaxy indoor leisure afternoon.
  6. 12 · Maple Leaf pools, early dinner and packing.
  7. 13 · Flight day. Breakfast, hotel checkout and prebooked transfer to HRB. See the shared departure plan below.

Getting around

Only move to Songbei if using both complexes. They are separate venues, not one resort; arrange rides between them and prebook the airport transfer from your final hotel.

Crowds & timing

Spa complexes can be busy on weekends. Swap the spa days to weekdays if avoiding crowds matters more than reducing transfers.

The trade-off

Check teen admission, height-based ticketing, shared swimwear areas and separate bathing areas. Massage, scrubs and food packages need individual confirmation.

05 🏮A quieter approach
HARBIN · DAOWAI SLOW DAYS

Old Harbin, one bite at a time

Historic courtyards, pastry tasting and long lunches, with warm indoor days between walks. A slower alternative to chasing every headline attraction.

7–12 Dec Harbin · 6 nights

For the teens · A snack-rating challenge, unusual architecture and an arcade break.

For the adults · Unhurried meals, photographs, beer and a midweek soak.

Explore places & the 6-night plan

中华巴洛克 · Chinese Baroque quarter

Old Daowai blends elaborate European-style street fronts with Chinese courtyard layouts. Wander a small cluster of lanes rather than trying to cover the whole district; allow 1–2 hours with a warm stop.

靖宇街老鼎丰 · Jingyu Street pastry stop

The heritage shop is a good anchor for a self-made tasting challenge. Choose a few pastries together, browse the little museum and take the winning flavour back to the hotel.

Make the rhythm local

Alternate short neighbourhood walks with sit-down meals, the Haxi arcade and a hot-spring day. The appeal is time to linger, rather than a new paid attraction every morning.

A suggested flow · December 2026

  1. 7 · BDH → Harbin; hotel and neighbourhood dinner.
  2. 8 · Chinese Baroque lanes and pastry tasting.
  3. 9 · Haxi arcade; teens choose the next meal.
