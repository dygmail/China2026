Where you'll be

Destination Guides

Weather, sights and practical notes for each stop.

Changchun / Jilin

2–3 Dec · Everyone · −18°C to −8°C

Arrival city and the road to the slopes, with rime ice at peak season.

Guide
  • Rime Ice (雾凇). On the Songhua River, a bucket-list winter spectacle unique to this region. Best viewed 07:00–09:00 along the Songhua River embankment. Free to view. Dress warmly!
  • Food. Explore local food — try Korean-Chinese 东北 cuisine!
  • Airport. Didi from Changchun Airport to city hotels: est. ¥60–90 (~S$11–17). Takes ~40 min.
  • Stay. Local 5-star hotels available. TBC per family. Agent quoted 2N Changchun at ~S$860/adult if bundled.
  • Leaving. The Beijing group's G136 departs Jilin on 7 Dec.

Club Med Beidahu

3–7 Dec · All 6 families · −15°C to −5°C

All-inclusive ski resort with excellent snow coverage, a hot spring and nightly shows.

Guide
  • Included. All meals & drinks, ski passes & lessons, Kids Club, Hot Spring (1 free entry/day).
  • First evening. Register on your first evening (3 Dec) at the Ski Sign-Up Counter, 18:00–20:00.
  • Ski gear. Ski gear is rented at Club Med.
  • Everything else. Open the full resort guide.

Changbaishan / Erdaobaihe

7–9 Dec · David & Joelle

Waterfalls, a green pond and an underground forest on Changbai Mountain.

Guide
  • Beauty Pine Sky Bridge Park 美人松空中廊桥. About 30 min.
  • Erdaobaihe town 二道白河. About 3 hr.
  • Changbai Waterfall 长白瀑布, Green Deep Pond 绿渊潭, Underground Forest 地下森林. About 1 hr each. Listed hours 07:30–18:00.
  • Stay. Lämpö Design Hotel, Building A56, Enduli, Baihe Street, Erdaobaihe Town.

Yanji

9–10 Dec · David & Joelle

One night of Korean-Chinese culture, street colour and market food.

Guide
  • Korean Folk Village 朝鲜族民俗园. About 1 hr.
  • College Town Wanghong Wall. About 30 min.
  • Yanji Xi Shichang market 西市场. Listed as 08:00–17:00.
  • Stay. Hampton by Hilton Yanji City Center 延吉希尔顿欢朋酒店, No. 336 Juzi Street, Yanji.

Harbin

10–13 Dec · David & Joelle

Russian-era streets, Siberian tigers, polar animals and an ice palace before flying home.

Guide
  • Saint Sophia Cathedral 圣索菲亚教堂, Songhua River 松花江, Zhongyang Street 中央大街. About 1 hr each.
  • Hongzhuan Morning Market 红专街早市. Closes 09:30.
  • Siberian Tiger Park 东北虎林园. 08:30–16:30.
  • Harbin Music Park's Music Corridor and Lao Daowai Baroque Museum, 10:00–12:00 and 13:00–20:00.
  • Harbin Polarland · Polar Pavilion 极地馆. About 3 hr, 09:00–17:00.
  • Ice and Snow Fantasy Palace. About 1 hr, 10:00–20:30 on Saturdays until 15 Dec.
  • Stay. Harbin Central Street Habsburg Hotel (Sofia Church), No. 184 Youyi Road, Tongjiang Subdistrict, Harbin.

Beijing

7–12 Dec · Five families · −5°C to 3°C

Universal Studios, the Great Wall and a dinner plan worth arriving hungry for.

Guide
  • Sights from Tricia's earlier plan, not yet given a day: Tiananmen Square, Forbidden City (closed Mondays), Temple of Heaven, Shichahai and the hutongs, Nanluoguxiang / Wudaoying Hutong. That plan's Great Wall section was Mutianyu 慕田峪.
  • Dinners. 花家怡园 (四合院总店), 八爷涮肉, 宫宴, 肥福排档 (北京总店), Peking duck (restaurant TBC).
  • Travel notes. Wear comfortable walking shoes · carry passport for entry · Dec temps −5°C to 5°C · Forbidden City closed Mondays.
  • Getting around. Beijing Didi rides: city centre ~¥25–60. Airport to city ~¥100–140 (~S$19–26). 20-seater bus ¥3,000 / day.
  • Stay. Local 5-star hotel if opting for Beijing leg. TBC per family.
